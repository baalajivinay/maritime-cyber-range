## Why the at-risk findings matter

**Remote mode hijack** — an attacker on the control network could abort or alter the mission at will -- e.g. force the vessel to hold, or drop out of its autonomous behaviour.

- *What to do:* require authenticated command-and-control (MAVLink2 message signing) and restrict which ground station the autopilot will obey.

**Remote control seizure** — an attacker could drive the vessel -- full authority over where it goes and how fast.

- *What to do:* lock the trusted ground-station ID (SYSID_MYGCS) and enable MAVLink2 signing so forged override commands are rejected.

**GPS position spoofing** — an attacker could walk the vessel off its intended track -- toward a hazard, out of a safe corridor, or into another vessel -- without touching the controls.

- *What to do:* cross-check GPS against a second, independent position source in the navigation filter (e.g. dead-reckoning/INS consistency gating); alarm on sudden position jumps.
